# PRO-MAX Beat Maker AI School

**BSS1815 PRO-MAX DMP**  
**DEVAN DEVAN NÈT — BATI POU JODI A — PARE POU DEMEN**

PRO-MAX Beat Maker AI School se yon lekòl dijital ki ede debutan, mizisyen, pwodiktè ak kreyatè aprann beat making, aranjman mizikal, mixing, mastering, mizik ayisyen ak itilizasyon entèlijans atifisyèl nan pwodiksyon mizik.

## Main Features

- 10 kou pwofesyonèl
- PRO-MAX AI Professor
- Egzamen pratik
- Suivi pwogrè elèv
- Sètifika final
- KRE, ENG ak FRA
- Responsive design pou telefòn, tablèt ak òdinatè
- Orange Apricot ak Black visual identity
- Koneksyon dirèk ak PRO-MAX Beat Maker

## Courses

1. Beat Making Fundamentals
2. BPM, Tempo & Rhythm
3. Drums & Percussion
4. Chords, Melody & Bass
5. Song Arrangement
6. Sampling & Sound Design
7. Mixing & EQ
8. Recording & Mastering
9. Haitian Music Production — Kompa, Rara, Rabòday & Twoubadou
10. AI Music Workflow
